How much does custom app development cost in 2026?

3 September 2026 · EBREGIS

One of the first questions any company asks when planning an app is also the hardest to answer outright: how much will it cost? The honest answer is «it depends», but that doesn't help you plan. Here we explain what really drives the price of a custom app.

Why there is no single price

A custom app is not an off-the-shelf product: it is built to solve a specific problem in your business. Two apps that look alike «from the outside» can cost very differently depending on what happens inside. The price is built by adding up decisions, and each one has a real impact on working hours.

The factors that set the budget

The number and complexity of features. An app that simply displays information is not the same as one with payments, real-time messaging or geolocation. Every feature adds design, development and testing.

The platforms. Android only, iOS only, or both. Cross-platform development is usually more efficient than building two separate native apps.

The design. A standard interface costs less than a carefully crafted brand design with animations and a highly polished experience.

The integrations. Connecting the app to your ERP, payment gateway or CRM adds technical work that is often underestimated.

The backend. Many apps need a server and a database behind them to manage users, content or real-time data.

Ongoing maintenance. An app doesn't end on launch day: budget for maintenance from the start.

Indicative ranges in Spain

As a general reference, a simple project starts at several thousand euros; a medium-complexity app with a backend and integrations sits in a noticeably higher range; and a complex platform can comfortably exceed those figures. These are orders of magnitude: the real number only emerges after analysing your case.

How to fit the budget

The most effective way to control cost is to start with an MVP (minimum viable product): the smallest version of the app that already delivers real value. You launch sooner, spend less up front and decide the next features with real usage data in hand.

In short

The price depends on features, platforms, design, integrations and maintenance. Rather than chasing a fixed number too early, define clearly what problem the app must solve and prioritise. At EBREGIS we build custom apps for iOS and Android, guiding you from idea to launch.
